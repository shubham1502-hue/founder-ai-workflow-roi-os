# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a respectful, practical, and inclusive project environment.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and professional language.
- Be direct about technical tradeoffs without personal attacks.
- Respect different levels of startup, operations, and engineering experience.
- Focus feedback on the work and the user impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private data, secrets, or confidential business information.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions away from the project scope.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate this code of conduct.
