# Where This Fits in Founder OS

Founder AI Workflow ROI OS is the AI leverage and workflow ROI layer in the broader founder operating system.

## Portfolio architecture

- AI leverage decisions: `founder-ai-workflow-roi-os`
- Pre-call GTM: `ai-gtm-command-center`
- Post-call sales intelligence: `founder-led-sales-call-os`
- Weekly revenue diagnosis: `founder-os-revenue-engine`
- Weekly operating review: `founder-weekly-operating-review-agent`
- Umbrella: `founder-os`

## How the systems work together

Use `founder-ai-workflow-roi-os` before buying tools, building automations, or hiring for operational load. It identifies which workflows have enough ROI and low enough risk to automate.

AI Agent Workflow Visibility Map sits inside Founder AI Workflow ROI OS. It is used after a workflow has been automated or agent-assisted. It helps founders inspect agent visibility, failure modes, human adoption, trust, and ROI before scaling the workflow.

Use `ai-gtm-command-center` for pre-call GTM preparation once GTM workflows are identified as high leverage.

Use `founder-led-sales-call-os` for post-call sales intelligence when sales calls and follow-up workflows are high leverage.

Use `founder-os-revenue-engine` to connect automation work to revenue bottlenecks.

Use `founder-weekly-operating-review-agent` to bring AI leverage work into the weekly operating review.

Use `founder-os` as the umbrella system that connects the company's operating cadence.

## Practical rule

This repo answers what to automate and what not to automate.

The other repos help execute specific parts of the operating system once that decision is clear.
